# Legal & Distribution Readiness Audit — Once Upon YOUR Child, powered by Story Weaver

Date: 2026-05-19
Brand note: the operator's intended customer-facing brand is **"Once Upon YOUR Child."** Today the in-app strings, the Privacy Policy, the Terms of Service, and the transactional emails all render **"Once Upon a Time"** instead — a brand drift introduced when the welcome-screen title was reworded to be more child-facing. The Stripe entity name and the premium product name (`Once Upon YOUR Child Enchanted`) still match the intended brand. The audit findings below treat "Once Upon YOUR Child" as the canonical brand and surface the in-product use of "Once Upon a Time" as its own alignment finding (L-ALIGN-07).
Method: Read-only review across legal documents, source code, and live web research against upstream vendor terms and store policies. Builds on (and does not re-litigate) the 2026-05-17 audit at `audit/LEGAL-COMPLIANCE.md`; net-new findings are flagged NEW, inherited findings reference the prior ID.
Scope: ToS, Privacy Policy, EULA, refund policy, parental consent, trademark, Apple App Store, Google Play Families, Gemini, OpenRouter, Stripe, and ancillary US/EU obligations (CCPA, EU AI Act Art. 50).
Output: this report and `04-legal-20260519-gap-checklist.csv`.

This audit is gap analysis, not legal advice. Several items are flagged "counsel required" and should not be acted on without a qualified attorney's review.

## Executive Summary

Eight Launch-Blockers stand between the codebase and a public app-store release. One is new and severe; four are inherited from the 2026-05-17 compliance audit; three are commercial/identity issues unique to this audit.

**The single most material new finding (L-AI-01).** The Gemini API Additional Terms of Service explicitly forbid building an API client "directed towards or is likely to be accessed by individuals under the age of 18." The Privacy Policy and welcome screen position this product as "for ages 3 to 17." Every story-generation, illustration, and photo-avatar code path uses Gemini. As of today the product violates the upstream provider's terms; Google may revoke API access, and shipping while in violation creates marketing-deception exposure independent of any regulator. This must be resolved (alternate provider, narrower audience, or written carve-out from Google) before any public submission.

**One trademark collision and one inadvertent-exposure brand drift (L-TM-01, L-TM-02).** "Story Weaver" — the technical/platform name retained on the launcher, in the repo, and on some receipts — is the canonical name of Pratham Books' StoryWeaver platform (storyweaver.org.in), a digital children's storytelling product in active global use since 2015. The customer-facing brand is intended to be "Once Upon YOUR Child," which is a far more distinctive mark and likely clearable; however, the app currently renders "Once Upon a Time" on the welcome screen, the consent screen, the Privacy Policy, the Terms, and the verification emails, putting it in inadvertent contact with Disney Reg. 4230524 and a crowded App Store space. The fix for L-TM-02 is largely a string sweep — revert in-product copy to the intended brand. A formal counsel clearance search on "Once Upon YOUR Child" and "Story Weaver" should still precede store submission.

**Inherited launch-blockers still open.** CMP-1 (`_kSkipEmailConsent=true` still ships), CMP-2 (`COPPA_REQUIRE_VERIFIED_CONSENT` defaults off in env), STORE-1 Phase 2 (IAP receipt verification stubs return 503; no products in App Store Connect / Play Console), STORE-10 (Android release keystore not generated — debug-signing fallback blocks Play upload).

**Unblocking sequence.**
1. Decide the Gemini path (alternate model, narrower audience, or written carve-out). Without resolution, every other launch step is wasted.
2. String-sweep "Once Upon a Time" → "Once Upon YOUR Child" across `lib/`, `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`, the in-app policy/terms screens, and `backend/utils/email_service.py`. Replace `assets/images/splash_logo.png` if it still reads "Once Upon YOUR Child" — wait, that is correct; the splash already matches the intended brand. Commission a formal counsel clearance search on "Once Upon YOUR Child" and "Story Weaver" before any store listing is created.
3. Flip `_kSkipEmailConsent=false`, set `COPPA_REQUIRE_VERIFIED_CONSENT=true` on Railway after a Resend round-trip smoke test (closes MT-166).
4. Generate the Android release keystore; the build infrastructure already supports it.
5. Complete IAP Phase 2 (Apple/Google credentials, receipt verification, store-side product setup) — 2–4 weeks, blocked on owner action.
6. Form a legal entity (LLC) and replace personal address/phone in the Privacy Policy with entity contacts.
7. Console-only steps: data-safety/privacy-label forms, age-rating questionnaire, Play gen-AI declaration.

Three of the eight blockers are dollars-and-paperwork, not engineering. The Gemini constraint is the only one that can stop everything else cold.
