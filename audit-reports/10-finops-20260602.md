# Cost & FinOps Audit — Unit Economics

**Project:** Story Weaver / "Once Upon YOUR Child"
**Date:** 2026-06-02
**Scope:** Variable cost attribution, per-story + per-subscriber unit economics, sensitivity to scale, cost-leak findings, optimization backlog.
**Method:** Static analysis of `C:\dev\story-weaver-app` (read-only). No production billing APIs queried. Token counts modeled from the authoritative per-band word-ceiling tables (`backend/services/story_service.py:21-130`) and prompt-assembly code, **not** from 30 live generations — the backend was not run and prod keys were not exercised (see *Estimation Method & Limitations*). External list prices taken from the repo's own `backend/services/cost_tracker.py:11-18` (marked "Verified") plus published Stripe / OpenRouter / Cloudflare schedules; flagged for re-verification where noted.

---

## Executive Summary

Story Weaver's variable cost is **not** dominated by AI text generation — that line is negligible (a fraction of a cent per story). The cost structure is dominated, in order, by:

1. **TTS narration (ElevenLabs).** At $0.18/1k characters, a single full narration of a medium story (~9,000 chars) costs **~$1.62** — roughly **1,000× the text-generation cost** of the same story and ~10× its illustration cost. A premium subscriber ($9.99/mo, ~$9.30 net of Stripe) who narrates as few as **six** medium stories on ElevenLabs in a month erases the entire margin. This is the single most important finding. It is partially guarded (free tier is locked to the on-device voice; paid TTS has a 20-call/day cap, a 500/hr rate limit, and a tiered fallback to Gemini Flash TTS → Edge → on-device), but the per-paid-user exposure on ElevenLabs is real and the default path is the most expensive provider.

2. **Illustration, conditionally.** The default provider is Cloudflare Workers AI Flux Schnell at **$0** — but only while under Cloudflare's fixed daily free Neuron pool. That pool is a step function: as DAU grows you exhaust it earlier each day and the marginal story's images cascade to Replicate ($0.003/img) and then OpenRouter Gemini Image ($0.0375/img — 12× Replicate). The DB-backed illustration cache (skips both the provider call and the quota on a hit) is the strongest mitigation already in place.

3. **Text generation fallback.** Gemini Flash text is ~$0.001/story. But the paid OpenRouter fallback model is **Claude Sonnet 4.7** at $3/$15 per M (×1.05 markup) — ~**50×** the Gemini cost. Frequent Gemini outages would silently multiply text cost.

Infrastructure (Railway, Redis, Postgres, Sentry) is well-controlled: every Redis key is TTL'd, Celery retries are bounded, and quotas fail closed on Redis outage. The two scale risks are the **solo-pool Celery worker** (throughput ceiling, needs horizontal scaling) and **Sentry frontend traces at 20%** (superlinear event cost beyond ~10k users).

**Bottom line:** unit economics are healthy at $9.99/mo *only if narration defaults off ElevenLabs*. The top optimization — make Gemini Flash TTS the default paid voice and gate ElevenLabs — is a 1–2 hour change that protects the entire monetization model.

**Top 5 optimizations (by dollars-saved-per-engineering-hour):**

| # | Optimization | Effort | Est. annual saving |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Default paid narration to **Gemini Flash TTS**; make ElevenLabs an opt-in "premium voice" or first-N-stories perk | 1–2 hr | ~70% of TTS spend (the largest variable line) |
| 2 | Swap paid OpenRouter fallback off **Claude Sonnet** to a cheap model (Gemini-via-OpenRouter or Llama 3.3 70B); only escalate after N Gemini failures | <1 hr | Caps a ~50× text-cost spike during Gemini outages |
| 3 | Reorder image cascade to prefer **Replicate before OpenRouter** (12× cheaper) and raise illustration-cache hit rate | 2–3 hr | Bounds image cost as Cloudflare free pool saturates |
| 4 | Drop Sentry **frontend `tracesSampleRate` 0.2 → 0.05** | 5 min | Avoids event-quota overage at 10k+ users |
| 5 | Reconcile the **two divergent cost tables** (delete stale `cost_tracking.py` rates) | 15 min | Prevents 33%-overstated internal cost decisions |

## 2. Per-Story Cost Model

### Token model (estimation method)

- **Output tokens** = band word-ceiling midpoint × **1.3 tokens/word** (English).
- **Input tokens** = assembled prompt (system scaffold + age-constraint `notes` block + character/companion/emotion context) ≈ **1,000–1,500 tokens**, rising with band (the `notes` blocks in `story_service.py` grow from ~40 words for Sprout to ~120 for Adult).
- Word ceilings are **measured** from `AGE_CONSTRAINTS` (`story_service.py:23-130`), not estimated.

### Text generation — Gemini Flash (paid), "medium" length

| Band | Words (mid) | Out tokens | In tokens | Cost/story |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sprout (3-4) | 375 | 488 | 1,000 | **$0.00022** |
| Explorer (5-7) | 775 | 1,008 | 1,100 | **$0.00038** |
| Adventurer (8-10) | 1,500 | 1,950 | 1,200 | **$0.00068** |
| Creator (11-13) | 2,200 | 2,860 | 1,300 | **$0.00096** |
| Adolescent (13-15) | 2,900 | 3,770 | 1,400 | **$0.00124** |
| Adult (medium) | 4,200 | 5,460 | 1,500 | **$0.00175** |

Free tier (Flash-Lite, half rate) is ~half of each. **Text is the cheapest line by three orders of magnitude.** Even the largest adult "long" story (~7,800 words) is ~$0.003.

**Fallback contrast:** the same Adventurer-medium story on the OpenRouter **Claude Sonnet** fallback costs `(1,200×3 + 1,950×15)/1e6 × 1.05 ≈ $0.034` — **~50× Gemini.**

### Illustration — by provider cascade (per story)

| Images/story | Cloudflare (default) | Replicate | OpenRouter | Gemini direct (BYOK) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 (ages 6+) | **$0.00** | $0.015 | $0.1875 | $0.20 (user-paid) |
| 10 (Sprout) | **$0.00** | $0.030 | $0.375 | $0.40 (user-paid) |

One image per page; non-BYOK single-image requests are **cache-first** (DB-backed, skips provider + quota on hit — `story_routes.py:1720-1769`). BYOK images are paid by the user's own key and not server-metered.

### TTS narration — full story (per story), chars ≈ words × 6

| Band (medium) | Chars | ElevenLabs ($0.18/1k) | Gemini ($0.054/1k) | Edge / on-device |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sprout (375w) | ~2,250 | **$0.405** | $0.122 | $0 |
| Adventurer (1,500w) | ~9,000 | **$1.62** | $0.486 | $0 |
| Adult (4,200w) | ~25,200 | **$4.54** | $1.36 | $0 |

**TTS on ElevenLabs is 1,000–4,000× the text cost and ~10–25× the image cost of the same story.** This is the unit-economics pivot.

## 3. Per-Subscriber Cost Model

Assumptions: monthly subscriber, churn-agnostic snapshot. Stripe fee per the published schedule.

### Free tier
- Text: capped 10/day, 100/mo on Flash-Lite → worst case ~$0.13/mo.
- Illustration: 10/mo (60/mo Sprout), default Cloudflare $0 → **~$0**.
- TTS: monthly chars = **0** → always on-device → **$0**.
- **Cost to serve a free user ≈ $0.00–0.15/mo.** Sustainable.

### Premium ($9.99/mo, ~$9.30 net of Stripe) — *typical* (20 stories, 5 images each, 5 narrated)
| Component | Path | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Text | 20 × ~$0.001 | $0.02 |
| Images | 100 imgs, mostly Cloudflare + some Replicate cascade | ~$0.06 |
| TTS | 5 × Gemini Flash ($0.486) | **$2.43** |
| Stripe | 2.9% + $0.30 | $0.59 |
| Infra share | amortized Railway/Redis/PG/Sentry | ~$0.50 |
| **Total** | | **~$3.60 → net +$5.70** |

### Premium — *heavy ElevenLabs narrator* (15 medium narrations on ElevenLabs)
- TTS: 15 × $1.62 = **$24.30** → **deeply negative**, bounded only by the 20-call/day cap and the global ElevenLabs plan budget. This is the failure mode F-01 protects against.

### Family ($19.99/mo, *unlimited* stories)
- Same TTS dynamics, **amplified by unlimited generation**. The "unlimited + ElevenLabs default" combination is the highest-risk cell in the entire model and must inherit the same TTS gating.

**Stripe effective rate by charge:** $9.99 monthly → 5.9%; $19.99 monthly → 4.4%; $79.99 annual → **3.3%**. Annual billing is materially better margin (F-07).

**Minimum sustainable price:** text+image ≈ $0.10/active user/mo; the binding variable is TTS. With narration defaulted to Gemini Flash and ~10 medium narrations/mo (~$4.86) + Stripe + infra, an engaged narrating premium user costs **~$6/mo**. $9.99/mo is sustainable **iff** ElevenLabs is gated; without that gate a single heavy narrator goes negative.

## 6. Optimization Backlog (ordered by $/eng-hour)

1. **F-01 — Gemini Flash TTS as paid default; gate ElevenLabs.** Highest dollar swing in the whole model; protects every paid tier. *Safety note: voice quality is a UX feature, not a safety control — Gemini Flash TTS is high quality; ElevenLabs becomes a premium perk, not a removal.*
2. **F-02 — cheapen the OpenRouter text fallback model.** Config-level; removes a latent ~50× spike.
3. **F-03 — already handled; future trigger only.** The cascade is already ordered cheapest-first and the account sits at 0/10k Neurons/day pre-launch — no code change. When daily Neurons approach 10k at scale, add the Workers Paid plan ($5/mo) so overage stays on Cloudflare ($0.00042/img) instead of cascading to Replicate (7×) / OpenRouter (89×). Tracked as MT-220.
4. **F-05 — Sentry frontend traces → 5%.** Trivial; prevents observability overage. Do **not** touch the `beforeSend` COPPA consent gate (`lib/main.dart:33-49`).
5. **F-04 — reconcile cost tables.** Trivial; restores trust in internal cost numbers.
6. **F-06 — TTS prewarm cache.** Medium effort; compounding benefit as session count grows.
7. **F-07 — annual-plan nudge.** Margin and churn benefit.

## Estimation Method & Limitations

- **Token counts are modeled, not measured from 30 live generations.** The audit prompt's preferred method (sample 30 stories, compute mean/p95 tokens) requires running the backend with live keys, which was out of scope for a static read-only pass. Instead, output tokens were derived from the **authoritative, measured** per-band word-ceiling tables (`story_service.py:23-130`) at 1.3 tokens/word, and input tokens from inspection of the prompt-assembly path. Because text cost is ~$0.001/story, even a 2× token-estimate error moves total unit economics by well under a cent — the conclusion (text is negligible; TTS dominates) is robust to this uncertainty.
- **External list prices** (Stripe 2.9%+$0.30; Claude Sonnet $3/$15) are published rates as of the knowledge cutoff and the repo's own "Verified" `cost_tracker.py` docstring. **Cloudflare Workers AI pricing was verified 2026-06-03** against Cloudflare's published pricing + the live account dashboard: 10k Neurons/day free, $0.011/1k Neuron overage, flux-1-schnell 9.6 Neurons/step × 4 steps = $0.00042/img, free pool ≈ 260 imgs/day; the account is on Workers Free at 0/10k Neurons/day and the app is **pre-launch** (no real traffic — every finding here is preventative, not a live bleed). Still **re-verify** the current ElevenLabs plan character allotment before acting on exact F-01 dollar figures — the *direction* of the finding holds regardless.
- **No production billing data** was used; all projections are model output with the assumptions stated, not measurements.

- No optimization recommends removing content-safety filters, the Gemini output safety filter, or the COPPA consent gate (Sentry `beforeSend`, `lib/main.dart:33-49`).
