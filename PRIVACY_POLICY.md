# Privacy Policy - Once Upon a Time, powered by Story Weaver

**Effective Date:** May 17, 2026

## Our Commitment to Privacy

Once Upon a Time (powered by Story Weaver) is committed to protecting the privacy and safety of children and families who use our storytelling platform. We believe that tools for children should be safe, transparent, and respectful of personal boundaries.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
- Email address (for parent/guardian accounts)
- Username and basic profile information
- Age range and preferences

### Usage Data
- Story creation patterns and preferences
- Activity engagement
- Character evolution progress
- App usage statistics

### Story & Feelings Data
- "Big feelings" a child chooses to share — a short, optional piece of text the child enters to shape the tone of a single story (for example, "I feel nervous about my first day at school"). This is used only to personalize that story. We treat it as transient story-personalization input, not as a health record or a retained emotional profile of your child.
- Story goals and themes
- Milestone achievements

### Character & Avatar Data
- Character names and avatar customization choices
- Photos (optional): if a parent enables photo-based avatars, a photo used to create a child's character avatar. Photo-based avatars are off by default and require parental opt-in. The photo is not stored on our servers.

## How We Use Information

### Storytelling Services
- Personalize stories and learning experiences
- Track progress and growth
- Provide age-appropriate content
- Generate stories, illustrations, and narration

### Platform Improvement
- Improve app functionality and user experience
- Develop new features
- Analyze usage patterns for better support
- Ensure platform safety and effectiveness

### Communication
- Send important updates about account and safety
- Provide progress summaries
- Share new features that may help your child

## AI-Generated Content

Stories, illustrations, and character avatars in Once Upon a Time are **generated by artificial intelligence (AI)**. We use third-party AI providers (listed in the Third-Party Services section below) to create this content from the details your child provides — such as a character name, chosen themes, story goals, and any "big feelings" the child shares.

What this means for families:

- **Content is machine-generated.** Stories and pictures are created automatically by AI. They are not written, drawn, or pre-approved by a human author or illustrator.
- **AI output can be imperfect.** AI is creative but not always accurate. It can occasionally produce content that is unexpected, factually wrong, or not quite right for your child. We apply safety filters, but no filter is perfect.
- **It is for entertainment, not advice.** Once Upon a Time is a storytelling app. AI-generated stories are not reviewed by a teacher, doctor, or other professional, and are not a substitute for professional care.
- **In-app labelling.** AI-generated stories and images carry a visible "Created with AI" label so it is always clear the content was produced by a machine.
- **Reporting content.** If you see AI-generated content that seems wrong or unsafe, you can report it from the story screen ("Report this content") or by emailing onceuponyourchild@gmail.com.

We encourage parents and guardians to review the stories their child creates.

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

- Data is encrypted in transit using TLS
- Data is stored on access-controlled servers
- Payment data and API keys are encrypted at rest
- Regular security reviews
- Limited data retention policies
- Access controls and monitoring

We do not provide end-to-end encryption. Story content and account data are processed on our servers so the app can function.

## Information Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent personal information to third parties. We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

- **With explicit parental consent** for the features the parent has enabled
- **Legal requirements** to ensure child safety
- **Emergency situations** where immediate help is needed
- **Sub-processors** listed in the Third-Party Services section below — each used only for the data and purpose described and under contractual data protection obligations

## Children's Privacy

Once Upon a Time is designed for children ages 3-17. We take special care with children's data:

- **Parental Control**: Parents/guardians have full access to their child's data
- **Verifiable Parental Consent**: For children under 13, a parent or guardian must provide verifiable consent before any personal data is collected (COPPA)
- **Age-Appropriate Content**: All features designed for child development
- **No Advertising**: We do not show ads or collect data for marketing
- **Educational Focus**: Data collection serves storytelling and educational purposes

## Parental Rights

Parents and guardians have complete control over their child's data:

- **Access**: View all data collected about their child
- **Correction**: Update or correct information
- **Deletion**: Request removal of data. To delete your child's data: open the app → tap the menu → **Parent Controls** → scroll to **Data & Privacy** → tap **Delete All My Data**. This permanently deletes your child's stories, characters and profile data and anonymizes your account so it can no longer be identified. You may also email onceuponyourchild@gmail.com to request deletion.
- **Export**: Request a copy of your child's data
- **Consent**: Control sharing and third-party access

## Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary:

- **Active accounts**: Data retained while the account is active
- **Inactive accounts**: Data deleted after 2 years of inactivity
- **Legal requirements**: Some data may be retained to comply with laws

## Third-Party Services (Sub-processors)

Once Upon a Time uses the third-party service providers below. We share only the minimum data required for each service to function. Each provider's own privacy policy governs its processing.

| Provider | Data category received | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Gemini** | A pseudonymized hero token (a non-identifying name used in place of the child's real name), story details, themes, any "big feelings" text the child shares, and image prompts | AI story-text and illustration generation |
| **OpenRouter** | Image prompts; on the photo-avatar path, the child's photo | Routes AI image-generation requests |
| **Replicate** | Image prompts; on the photo-avatar path, the child's photo | AI image and avatar generation |
| **Cloudflare Workers AI** | Image prompts; on the photo-avatar path, the child's photo | AI image and avatar generation |
| **ElevenLabs** | Generated story text | Text-to-speech narration |
| **Stripe** | Parent payment information (handled directly by Stripe; we never store full card details) | Subscription and payment processing |
| **Railway** | All app data — profiles, stories, preferences (stored in the United States) | Cloud hosting and infrastructure |
| **Firebase / Google Analytics** | Anonymized app usage events (consent-gated, off by default, not enabled for children under 13) | App analytics |
| **Sentry** | Crash and error diagnostics | Error monitoring and stability |
| **Resend** | Parent/guardian email address | Sending COPPA parental-consent verification emails |

The exact AI image provider used (Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Replicate, or Cloudflare Workers AI) may vary by build and availability.

**Photo avatars (optional).** If you choose to create a character avatar from a photo, the photo is sent to our servers and then to the active AI image provider solely to generate the cartoon avatar. It is not stored on our servers, is not used for advertising or any other purpose, and is not sold or shared. Photo-based avatars are turned off by default and require a parent or guardian to opt in.

## Not a Health or Therapeutic Product

Once Upon a Time is a storytelling and entertainment app with an emotional-wellbeing angle — it helps children explore feelings through personalized stories. It is **not** a therapeutic, clinical, or medical product, and it does not diagnose, treat, or provide care for any emotional or mental-health condition. The "big feelings" a child shares are used only to personalize a story; they are not retained as a health profile. Always consult a qualified professional for serious emotional or mental-health concerns.
