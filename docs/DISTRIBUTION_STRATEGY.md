# Distribution Strategy — the B2B2C Therapist/Counselor Wedge

**Date:** 2026-07-04 · **Author:** Fable 5 strategy pass (session/fable-docs)
**Status:** SHELF-READY PLAN. Launch is deliberately paused (owner decision).
Nothing here commits to a date; Phase 0 can be executed pre-launch, everything
else waits for the green light.

## 1. The problem this doc solves

The six-hats money assessment (2026-07-03) concluded: **product risk LOW,
distribution risk HIGH and unsolved.** A passive app-store listing earns
approximately nothing. This doc turns the "B2B2C wedge" idea into a concrete,
costed, solo-dev-sized plan, built on primary-source research into how
comparable SEL/therapeutic kids' products actually got distribution.

**Core thesis:** don't compete for parents in the open app store. Reach them
through the professionals they already trust — child therapists, play
therapists, school counselors — with a product positioned as the clinician's
**between-session tool**, not a therapy replacement. Our compliance/safety
depth and therapeutic framing are worth little to an app-store browser but are
exactly what this channel screens for.

## 2. What the comparables actually did (research findings)

Full sourced briefs live with the session records; the load-bearing patterns:

1. **Founder outreach, not ads, is the universal $0 engine.** Slumberkins
   (therapist-founded, $200 start → millions) ran **zero paid advertising for
   ~3 years**; the engine was 2–3 hours *nightly* of personal, gift-first DMs
   to parenting/educator micro-influencers. Wonderbly's first 20k books:
   mommy-blogger outreach. Every big unlock (Apple TV+, retail, funding) came
   *after* organic traction, never as the starting move.
2. **Free-to-professional is the proven wedge that converts to family
   revenue.** Moshi: free for all educators, families pay $9.99/mo. Closegap:
   free-forever for schools → 200-300k students by word of mouth. GoNoodle:
   teacher-ambassador program.
3. **Clinician-referral mechanics are small builds, not businesses.** The
   validated pattern (nOCD, Little Otter, Manatee): a frictionless referral
   form + free printable handouts/toolkits as the goodwill carrot. **Nobody
   pays clinicians commissions**, and nobody even gives free full accounts —
   so offering clinicians free accounts is a *differentiator*, not table
   stakes.
4. **The credibility bar at our scale is ONE named clinician + a self-serve
   CASEL one-pager.** Slumberkins' entire "experts" page is its own two
   founders. Mightier names no individual advisor. CASEL alignment PDFs
   (Panorama, Second Step format: checkmark matrix, 5 competencies × product
   features) are **self-published marketing docs CASEL never reviews** — $0.
   Even Slumberkins hasn't published one → open lane, and it closes our one
   identified credentialing gap (see SEL positioning memo).
5. **What NOT to buy:** CASEL Program Guide registry ($1,750 + requires an
   n≥100 RCT delivered as a school-day curriculum — structurally mismatched
   for an app); ASCA Alliance Partners (College Board/Sallie Mae tier);
   any paid acquisition.

## 3. Channel map (concrete, costed)

### School counselors — ASCA
- ~43,000 members; annual conference ~5,000 counselors (2026: July 11–14,
  New Orleans). Exhibit floor entry ≈ **$2,300** (10×10 + mandatory carpet)
  + travel; cheapest validated entries are **$599–699 headline ads**
  (Aspects newsletter / conference e-blasts, ~43–45k reach) and **$999**
  state-association newsletters (46k reach, 60% non-members).
- No free directory exists — ASCA visibility is pay-to-play at every tier.
  **Verdict: later-stage channel.** Don't spend here until therapist-pilot
  testimonials exist.

### Play therapists — APT
- *Play Therapy* magazine + weekly newsletter reach ~7,700; conference
  ~1,100 attendees; **booth $550–600** (incl. 3 badges + app listing),
  mobile-app banner **$350**. Dramatically cheaper per-contact than ASCA and
  a tighter fit for the therapeutic framing.
- **Podcasts are the open door:** Lessons from the Playroom (Lisa Dion,
  139k FB reach), Dr. Brenna Hicks' two shows, Play Therapy Community
  (Jackie Flynn). Direct-pitch guests wanted; the hook: "AI storytelling,
  COPPA-hardened, therapist-designed between-session practice."

### Certification signal
- **kidSAFE LISTED seal** explicitly targets budget startups (pricing
  unpublished — get a quote; budget ≤$1,000). We already exceed the
  underlying compliance bar (see COPPA gap analysis), so this is
  badge-purchase, not remediation. Skip PRIVO/ESRB/TRUSTe.

## 4. Pricing implications

- Non-clinical SEL app norm: **$9–13/mo or $60–90/yr family** (Moshi 9.99,
  Joon 12.99, GoZen 197/yr). "Therapeutic-grade" positioning (Mightier)
  supports $28–40/mo — our clinical framing may earn a premium tier later,
  but launch at the app norm.
- **Clinician accounts: free, full-featured** (differentiator per §2.3),
  plus printable session handouts + family referral codes.
- School/practice site license (later): **$200–350/yr** — the
  teacher-expensable, procurement-free sweet spot (Better Kids $350,
  Slumberkins Curriculum Hub $199).

## 5. The plan

### Phase 0 — pre-launch, can start now (~$0–500, no publish required)
1. **Credibility kit.** CASEL-5 checkmark matrix mapping Feelings Wheel /
   Life Quests / age bands / boundary-skills content to the five
   competencies (Second Step format) + ASCA Mindsets & Behaviors mapping.
   The clinical-review packet (docs/CLINICAL_REVIEW_ADOLESCENT_ANTIHERO.md)
   and the antihero evidence batch are ready-made evidence citations.
2. **Recruit ONE named clinical advisor** (LMFT/LCSW/PsyD): free lifetime
   access + named credit (+ small stipend if needed). One person is the
   whole bar at this scale. This same person can clear the outstanding
   clinical sign-offs (antihero band, crisis flow) — two birds.
3. **kidSAFE LISTED quote** (email; costs nothing to ask).

### Phase 1 — first 90 days post-green-light (~$350–1,000)
4. **10-therapist free pilot**, nOCD/Manatee-style: sign-up form, free full
   accounts for therapist + client-family codes, 2–3 printable handouts
   ("using a personalized story between sessions"). Exit ask per therapist:
   a testimonial + a micro-survey → a citable small-n outcomes line
   (Better Kids' "60% of teachers, n=750" is the model; even n=10 beats
   Slumberkins' zero published evidence).
5. **Nightly founder outreach** (the Slumberkins engine): 1–2 hrs/night of
   personal, gift-first DMs/emails to play-therapy podcast hosts and
   parent/therapist micro-creators. Pitch the podcasts as a guest.
6. Optional paid toe-in-water once testimonials exist: APT app banner
   ($350) or one ASCA headline ad ($699).

### Phase 2 — after pilot signal (not before)
7. APT booth ($550–600 + travel) with pilot testimonials on the table.
8. Free-for-counselors tier formalized (Closegap/Moshi wedge); apply to
   Fast Forward / Headstream accelerators (free, no equity).
9. School/practice site license at $200–350/yr.

**Total Phase 0+1 spend: ~$350–1,850.** The engine is time, not money —
consistent with every comparable studied.

## 6. Risks / honest caveats

- **The binding constraint is founder hours, not dollars.** The Slumberkins
  engine was nightly manual outreach for years. If that cadence isn't
  sustainable (solo, health considerations), Phase 1 stretches — the plan
  still works, slower. Design the pilot to be async (form + email drip).
- AI-generated content will meet skepticism in clinical communities —
  lead with the safety architecture (two-layer moderation, fail-closed for
  minors, COPPA depth, named advisor), not the AI.
- Comparable claims above rest on public sources of mixed quality; treat
  revenue figures as directional. Flagged-as-snippet items were not
  verified against primary pages.
- Everything here waits for the owner's launch green-light. Phase 0 items
  create no public commitment and can be done while paused.
