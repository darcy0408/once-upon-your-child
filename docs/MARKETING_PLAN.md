# Marketing Plan — Once Upon YOUR Child

*Drafted 2026-07-03. Constraints: solo founder, $0 budget, a few hours/week, launch deliberately paused (nothing here triggers launch or monetization until Darcy green-lights it).*

---

## 1. Positioning (worked example — confirm this shape first)

**One-liner:**
> Once Upon YOUR Child turns your kid into the hero of stories that actually teach something — personalized, illustrated tales that help children practice feelings, boundaries, and brave choices.

**Who we're for (in priority order):**
1. **Parents of kids 3–12** who want screen time that does emotional work — bedtime, big feelings, new-sibling moments, "we need to talk about boundaries."
2. **Therapists, counselors, SLPs, teachers** looking for personalized SEL material (secondary — they're the credibility engine, not the revenue engine at first).
3. **Grandparents/gift-givers** (later; gift framing works once the product has reviews).

**Three messaging pillars** (every piece of content maps to one):

| Pillar | Message | Proof we have / need |
|---|---|---|
| **It's THEIR story** | Your child by name, their face, their pet, their fears — not a template with a name swapped in | Product demo; avatar + companion + saga fields |
| **Stories that do something** | SEL is the spine: boundary-setting, "uh-oh feeling," being-seen arcs — not empty entertainment | NEED: CASEL 5 mapping doc + named advisory clinician (see §2, this closes the Slumberkins gap) |
| **Safety built in, not bolted on** *(reworded from "Built like it's for kids, because it is" — M-1, docs/DECISION_D1_D2_KIDS_CATEGORY_ANALYTICS_2026-07-13.md: avoid "for kids" audience framing in listing/marketing copy, Apple guideline 2.3.8)* | COPPA-compliant, parent-consent flows, moderated content, no ads, no data sold | Consent system, moderation layer, privacy policy; kidSAFE seal later |

**What we never do in marketing:** target children directly (parents only — COPPA discipline extends to ads/content), overclaim therapy ("supports SEL," never "treats anxiety"), or use real kids' generated stories without explicit parental permission.

## 2. Phase A — Pre-launch groundwork (start now, $0)

Everything here is safe to do while the launch stays paused, and compounds while waiting.

| # | Asset | Why | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Positioning block above → landing page copy** | One page at onceuponyourchild.app: one-liner, 3 pillars, screenshots, email capture ("Get notified at launch") | 2–3 hrs once |
| A2 | **Email waitlist** | The only marketing asset you own outright. Free tier of Buttondown/MailerLite; Resend stays transactional-only | 1 hr once |
| A3 | **CASEL 5 / ASCA mapping doc + advisory clinician** | Closes the one flank competitors (Slumberkins) are ahead on; becomes a PDF parents/therapists can cite | 3–4 hrs (I can draft the mapping) |
| A4 | **SEO content seeds** — 4–6 posts | Long-tail parent searches: "stories to teach kids boundaries," "personalized bedtime stories," "SEL stories for 6 year olds." Slow channel; start early | 1 post / 2 wks |
| A5 | **ASO prep** — store listing copy, keyword list, screenshots | Ready to paste the day the Play pause lifts; zero launch-day scramble | 2 hrs once |
| A6 | **Beta family testimonials** | 5–10 real families → quotes + first reviews queued. Friends/family + one parenting community ask | ongoing |
| A7 | **Outreach list** — 20 parent micro-creators, teacher/SLP creators, kid-tech newsletters | Built now, contacted at launch | 30 min / wk |

**Weekly cadence (≈3 hrs):** 1 hr writing (A4), 1 hr community presence — genuinely participate in 2–3 parenting/teacher spaces, no spam (feeds A6/A7), 1 hr asset work (A1→A2→A3→A5, in order).

## 3. Phase B — Launch plan (executes only on green-light)

**T-minus 2 weeks:** store listing live in draft (A5), waitlist teaser email, line up beta families to leave honest reviews week one, send embargo-style notes to the A7 list.

**Launch week:**
1. Waitlist email → install link (this list is the whole reason Phase A exists).
2. Personal-story post in the 2–3 communities where you've built presence — founder story ("I built this for my own kid") outperforms product pitches.
3. Creator outreach: free premium access for the A7 list, no payment, disclosure required.
4. Product Hunt / betalist-style launch (optional, one morning's effort, indie-friendly audience).

**First 90 days — one loop, repeated:**
- **Reviews are the growth engine** for a kids' app: in-app "enjoying this?" prompt → store review (parents only, post-consent). Target: 50 reviews by day 90.
- Keep the A4 content cadence; each post ends with the app as the answer.
- Ask every happy beta family for one referral. Manual > building a referral feature.
- Monthly: check ASO keywords against actual store search terms, adjust listing.

**Explicitly deferred (revisit at ~1k installs):** paid ads, TikTok/Reels grind, school/district partnerships, PR agencies, affiliate programs.

## 4. Budget (~$50/mo available)

**Rule: bank it, don't drip it.** $50/mo trickled into ads buys ~25–50 clicks/month — too little to ever produce a signal. Instead:

- **Phase A: spend $0.** Free tiers cover landing page, waitlist, analytics. Let the budget accumulate (~$200–400 by launch).
- **At launch, two concentrated tests:**
  1. One sponsorship slot in a small parenting newsletter ($50–200) — founder story + launch link; doubles as message testing.
  2. One 5–7 day Meta ads burst ($150–200) targeting **parents** (never children), run only after the store listing has screenshots + first reviews.
- **Deferred spends:** kidSAFE seal (hundreds of $, post-traction), any recurring tool subscriptions, always-on ads.

## 5. Measures that matter (pre-revenue)

- Waitlist size (Phase A north star — even 100 emails makes launch day real)
- Beta families active weekly + testimonial count
- Organic search impressions on the landing page (Cloudflare analytics, free)
- Post-launch: installs/week, store rating, review count, D7 retention
