## Accomplished
- Diagnosed and fixed the robotic-voice issue on the Sprout avatar wizard photo step. Root cause: phrase "Let's take a photo of your face with a grown-up!" was the 23rd entry in `kWarmUpPhrases` — at ~1.5s sequential prewarm, that's ~35s before cached, but a child can tap through Sprout's 3-step flow (welcome → gender → favoriteColor → photo) well before then. The live `speak()` call would race the in-flight prewarm and any failure (likely 429 from rate-limit contention) drops to the on-device flutter_tts fallback. Fix: reordered `lib/services/app_tts_service.dart:30-55` so Sprout avatar wizard prompts (gender, favoriteColor, photo, "magical hero is ready") and color tap echoes are at the top of the queue. Now cached within ~5s of app start instead of ~35s. Picked up and committed by parallel session as `3d73b6b5` (combined with their CanvasKit checkerboard fix for MT-027).
