- **`f67c8981`** — Resume/continue feature so a child can't lose a story:
  - `StoryResultScreen` bookmarks the current page to SharedPreferences (`story_progress_<id>`) on every flip; re-opening a saved story resumes there; the bookmark clears at end-of-story.

  - New "Continue your story" card at the top of the Stories home (most recent story, last 30 days) — one tap back in.
  - Tapping a character with a recent story opens a Continue / Start-a-new-story bottom sheet instead of silently launching a fresh wizard.
  - The storybook cover's dead "tap the arrow" hint (a decorative `Icon` with no handler — the bug the user hit) is replaced with a real, pulsing, working "Start Reading" button; the cover image is now tappable too.
