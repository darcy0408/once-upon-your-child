## Section 2 — The three pillars (one block each)

### It's THEIR story
Not a template with a name swapped in. Your child's name, their look, their companion animal, the thing they're nervous about this week — woven into a story written just for them. Kids lean in when the hero is *them*.

### Stories that do something
Every story has a spine: recognizing the "uh-oh feeling," saying no and meaning it, being seen when you feel invisible. Entertainment that leaves something behind — the language kids need for their next hard moment.

### Safety built in, not bolted on

## Section 3 — How it works (3 steps)

1. **Tell us about your hero.** Name, age, what they love, what they're working on — takes two minutes.
2. **Pick tonight's adventure.** Choose a theme or a feeling to practice; we write and illustrate a story that fits your child's age exactly.
3. **Read it together.** A story that's theirs — and a conversation that starts itself.

**Age note (small text):** Stories adapt to three age bands — Sprout (3–5), Explorer (6–8), and Adventurer (9–12) — so a five-year-old and an eleven-year-old get genuinely different stories, not the same one with bigger words.

## Section 5 — Short FAQ

**Is this safe for my child's data?**
Yes — parental consent comes first, we don't run ads or sell data, and the app is built for COPPA compliance. Full details in our privacy policy.

**What ages is it for?**
3–12, with stories genuinely written for your child's age band — not one story stretched across all ages.

**Does my child use it alone?**
It's parent-led. You set it up, you approve, you read together (older kids can read solo once you say so).

**What does it cost?**
There will be a free tier at launch. Waitlist members hear pricing first.

**When does it launch?**
Soon — join the waitlist and you'll get exactly one email when it's live.

## Copy rules applied (for future edits)

- Parents are the audience, always — never written to children (COPPA discipline).
- "Helps practice / supports" language only — never "treats," "therapy," or clinical claims.
- No features promised that may be launch-gated (e.g., voice narration is NOT mentioned — TTS is under review for under-13 use).
- Every section maps to a §1 pillar from MARKETING_PLAN.md.
