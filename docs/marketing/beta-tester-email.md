# Beta Tester Invitation Email — Ready to Share

Companion to `docs/marketing/clinician-outreach-emails.md` (clinician outreach) — this one is
the **general invited-tester ask**: friends, family, professors, other parents. Personalize the
`[brackets]`. Staged 2026-07-14; first send = Darcy's English Lit professor (personalized
variant with a prose-critique paragraph, staged as a Gmail draft).

Ground rules baked into the email:
- **Invited, not public** — explicit "please don't post the link publicly yet" line (COPPA
  launch gates / MT-310; public promotion waits for the flag flips + sign-offs).
- Free tier only (5 stories/mo) — testers never need a card.
- Feedback ask is concrete: confusion points, expectation mismatches, personalization quality,
  willingness-to-pay gut check. Half-formed notes encouraged.

---

> **Subject: Would you try my kids' storytelling app and tell me what's confusing? (~10 minutes)**
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I've spent the past year and a half building Once Upon YOUR Child — an app that creates
> personalized stories for kids ages 3–17. Each story stars the child (their name, their
> interests, an avatar that looks like them) and quietly practices a social-emotional skill
> like naming feelings, calming down, or setting boundaries.
>
> It's live now, and before I open it to the public I'm asking a small group of people I
> trust to try it and tell me the truth.
>
> If you have ten minutes:
> 1. Go to https://onceuponyourchild.app (phone or computer browser — nothing to install)
> 2. Create a character — a real kid or an invented one, either works
> 3. Generate a story or two (free — no card, no catch)
>
> Then just reply and tell me:
> - Where did you get confused or stuck, even for a second?
> - Did anything behave differently than you expected?
> - Did the story feel like it was actually about *your* character, or could it have been
>   anyone's?
> - Honest gut check: would a parent you know pay $9.99/month for this?
>
> Half-formed notes are exactly what I want — "this button confused me" is gold. Screenshots
> welcome.
>
> One ask: please don't post the link publicly yet. I'm keeping this to an invited group
> until launch.
>
> Thank you — this genuinely helps.
>
> Darcy Van Pelt
> Founder, Once Upon YOUR Child
> (970) 640-2011 · darcy0408@gmail.com
